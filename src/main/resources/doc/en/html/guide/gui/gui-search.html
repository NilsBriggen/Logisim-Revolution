Find Action

Find Action searches everything Logisim can do from one text field. Open it with Ctrl-Shift-A (Cmd-Shift-A on macOS), by tapping Shift twice, with the search button in the activity bar, or with Help → Find Action. Type a few letters of what you are looking for; the letters need not be next to each other, so expim finds Export Image.

What it searches

Results are grouped by kind, under a heading that gives the number of matches. The group holding the best match comes first.

  • Menus - every menu command. Commands that are unavailable just now are shown dimmed.
  • Circuits - the circuits and VHDL entities of the project. Choosing one opens it.
  • Add - the components of the loaded libraries, and of the built-in libraries not loaded yet. Choosing one selects it for placing, loading its library first if needed.
  • Placed Components - the components already placed in the project's circuits, found by their label or by their type: clk finds the pin labelled clk, RAM finds every RAM. Each result shows the circuit it is in and its type, or its position when it has no label. Choosing one opens its circuit, selects it and scrolls it into view.
  • Settings - every page and every setting of the Preferences window and of the Project Options window, with the setting's current value on the right. A setting is also found by one of its choices or by words from its page or section, so dark finds the Theme setting. Choosing a setting opens its window at its page, scrolls to it, outlines it briefly and gives it the keyboard focus.
  • Recent Files - the files opened recently.

Before anything is typed the list shows the menus, circuits, components to add and settings pages, as an index to browse. Placed components and individual settings appear only once you type.

Using the keyboard

Up and Down move through the results, stepping over the group headings and wrapping around at either end; Page Up and Page Down move ten rows at a time. Enter runs the selected result and Esc closes the window.

Each group shows its best few results. When there are more, the last row of the group reads Show N more: select it and press Enter, or click it, to list the whole group.

Tapping Shift twice can be turned off in the Window page of the Preferences, and the Ctrl-Shift-A shortcut can be changed in its Hotkey settings page.
